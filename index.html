Introduction

I am a programming instructor with a background in engineering and physics. My special interest is in modeling physical and social phenomena, with a strong side interest in business process modeling with Simio.
My most important work is a set of Avalonia-based apps which will -hopefully- be used by students and researchers to design computer-based psychology experiments. These apps and the C# library of model classes they use are in HurPsy project, which is hosted here, on GitHub.
HurPsy will be made freely available to help non-programmers to design psychology experiments without writing code.
